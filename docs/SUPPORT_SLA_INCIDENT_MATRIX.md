# Support SLA & Incident Matrix

| Severity | Example | Response target | Escalation |
|---|---|---:|---|
| SEV1 | data exposure, global outage, payment corruption | 15 min | immediate owner + rollback |
| SEV2 | critical feature degradation | 60 min | operational owner |
| SEV3 | localized functional issue | 1 business day | product/support |

All incidents require tenant, provider, timeline and resolution evidence; secrets and credentials never enter support logs.
