# M6 — Support SLA and Incident Matrix

## Severity
- SEV1: global outage, data exposure, incorrect charge, widespread access failure.
- SEV2: major degradation of a critical capability.
- SEV3: localized functional issue without critical impact.

## Target response
- SEV1: <= 15 minutes, 24/7 operational coverage.
- SEV2: <= 60 minutes during support coverage.
- SEV3: <= 1 business day.

## Evidence
Every incident records tenant, severity, start time, detection source, owner, mitigation, resolution and post-incident action.
