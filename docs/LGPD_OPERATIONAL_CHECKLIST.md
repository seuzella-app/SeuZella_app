# LGPD Operational Checklist

## Required before commercial go-live
- Privacy policy published.
- Terms of use published.
- Data controller/operator roles documented.
- Consent/other lawful-basis capture mapped by purpose.
- Data export procedure tested.
- Deletion request procedure tested.
- Retention policy documented.
- Incident notification procedure documented.
- Subprocessor/DPA inventory maintained.
- Support procedure for data-subject requests documented.
