# M6 — Commercial KPI Contract

## Core metrics
- MRR and ARR by billing plan.
- Net revenue after gateway fees/refunds.
- Payment success and failed-renewal rates.
- CAC and payback period from dedicated marketing-cost records.
- Active tenants and tenant retention.
- Pilot conversion rate.
- Support response SLA attainment.
- Customer health distribution.
- Reservation and automation success rate.

## Rule
Every KPI must state its source, period, calculation and tenant scope. No KPI may silently mix SaaS billing with guest reservation revenue.
