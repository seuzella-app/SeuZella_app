# M6 — Customer Success Playbook

## First 30 days
- Day 0: onboarding and operational handoff.
- Day 1: verify reservations, payments, notifications and access automation.
- Day 7: review usage, incidents, unanswered workflows and owner feedback.
- Day 14: review financial reconciliation and unused capabilities.
- Day 30: success review and expansion/retention decision.

## Health signals
- active usage;
- reservation/payment success rate;
- WhatsApp response success;
- automation coverage;
- support incidents;
- owner adoption.

## Goal
Customer Success measures business outcome and operational stability, not feature count.
