# Go-Live Readiness Matrix

## Product
- Core customer journey documented.
- Pricing and billing rules canonical.
- Onboarding and support playbooks available.

## Legal / compliance
- Terms and privacy publication verified.
- LGPD operational procedures documented.
- Supplier/customer fiscal responsibilities explicit.

## Operations
- Incident runbook.
- Rollback path.
- Backup/restore evidence.
- Monitoring and alerts.

## Commercial
- First-pilot acceptance complete.
- Billing activation verified.
- Customer support owner assigned.
- Customer success handoff documented.
