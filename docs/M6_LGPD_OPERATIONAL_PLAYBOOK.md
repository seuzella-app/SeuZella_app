# M6 — LGPD Operational Playbook

## Required operational controls
- Consent and legal basis recorded where required.
- Data-subject request intake and identity verification.
- Export and deletion workflows with audit evidence.
- Retention policy by data class.
- Processor/subprocessor inventory.
- Incident response and notification decision path.
- Access logging for sensitive tenant data.

## Boundary
The product automates the operational workflow; legal responsibility and final legal interpretation remain with the configured controller/operator and legal/accounting advisors.
