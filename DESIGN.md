# DESIGN.md — SEU ZÉLLA OFFICIAL DESIGN SYSTEM

## Brand & Aesthetic Overview
- **Brand:** Seu Zélla (`seuzella.com`)
- **Theme:** Dark Mode Obsidian & Hospitality High-Contrast
- **Aesthetic:** Dark Glassmorphism, Micro-borders, Emerald Operational Accents (`#10b981`), Cyan Pulse (`#06b6d4`)
- **Typography:** Inter / Geist / Outfit, 140% scaling on critical PIN/price metrics for mobile readability
- **Primary Payment Actions:** PIX 1-Clique (Asaas & Mercado Pago) with instant confirmation badge
- **Core Surfaces:**
  - Background: `#09090f`
  - Cards: `#12121a` / `#181826` with `rounded-3xl` and `border border-zinc-800/80`
  - Operational Active Pill: `100% OPERACIONAL` with pulsing emerald dot (`#10b981`)
